Auto Rec

You don't have to press record.

.Capture follows the camera's REC. Pick the method that fits your signal path and camera, so you don't miss takes even when the record flag isn't available.

SDI Rec — Record flag

Follow the camera's record flag

Reads the record flag the camera embeds in SDI and starts and stops with it. The most reliable method with cameras that send it, such as ARRI and Sony VENICE. Works with AJA devices (io 4K, io X3, io XT).

ROI Rec — Visual detection

Watch the REC indicator on screen

Detects the color change of the REC indicator in the monitor picture. Find REC ● registers its position and lit color in one click, and keeps tracking even when an on-set LUT shifts the red. Presets for ALEXA 35 / Mini / Mini LF / AMIRA / VENICE / VENICE 2 / FX6 / BURANO / Ronin 4D. Works with any device, including Blackmagic.

Bridge — Wireless

Trigger from across the set

Follows the REC signal received by a USB-connected Bridge receiver (M5Stack). Carries the DIT's record start all the way to an offline Mac, wirelessly.

Premiere Pro

Stop recording, and it's already in the bin.

The included CEP panel ".Capture Sync" imports every finished take into a bin named after your project. The editor just waits for the next cut.

Metadata carried over
Scene / Shot / Take, comments and star ratings come across as they are. Four stars or more are marked Good, and IN / OUT points set during recording are applied to the clip.
Renames follow
Fix the Scene / Take or the RH / HS tag after recording, and both the file and Premiere's link follow automatically. Names like 12_C003_T002RH.mov stay intact.
Auto copy to the edit SSD
After each take, copy it to another volume and have Premiere import the copy. Keep the capture drive and the edit drive separate.
SDI OUT handover
When Premiere outputs directly to your device, .Capture keeps the input and hands only the SDI output to Premiere while it is in front, so Mercury Transmit works and recording keeps running. A red blink in the menu bar shows the recording state from any app.

.Capture Transmit — New in 1.3

Send Premiere's picture out through .Capture.

Choose ".Capture" as Premiere's output device. Premiere's picture and sound go out of SDI OUT through .Capture, so headphones and SDI play together, and recording never stops. Works with AJA and Blackmagic. The plugin installs from the first-launch prompt in one click (your Mac password is asked once).

Audio — SDI + Headphones

SDI and headphones at once

Premiere alone sends sound to only one of them. .Capture plays it on the SDI monitor and your Mac's headphones at the same time, with picture, headphones and SDI lined up.

Recording — Always on

Keeps recording while you edit

.Capture holds the input the whole time you work in Premiere, so LIVE and Auto Rec keep running in the background. No more handing an AJA device back and forth with Premiere.

SDI OUT — Last used

Shows whichever you used last

SDI OUT keeps showing .Capture or Premiere, whichever was in front last. Clicking your browser doesn't switch it. 9:16 vertical sequences fit into SDI with black sides.

Specifications

Recording formats
Apple ProRes 422 Proxy / LT / 422 / HQ / 4444 / 4444 XQ (.mov, 48 kHz 2 ch, with a timecode track). H.264 (.mp4) and BWF (.wav) can be generated in the background for every take.
Devices
Blackmagic UltraStudio / DeckLink (Desktop Video required), AJA io / KONA (AJA driver required), NDI sources on the network
Frame rates
Follows the input: 23.976 / 24 / 25 / 29.97 / 50 / 59.94 and more. 23.976 is recorded exactly, not rounded to 24.00.
HD frame
For Open Gate or feeds with status overlays: draw a 16:9 frame on the frame lines and .Capture scales the recording so that area becomes exactly 1920×1080 (the OSD outside is kept). Drop the clip into an HD sequence and the picture fits.
Language
English / Japanese (follows the system, or choose in Settings).
